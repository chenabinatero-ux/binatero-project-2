About Us

A Catholic Institution Dedicated to Excellence

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. (KCC) is an educational institution serving the community of Kabankalan City and its neighboring towns. Guided by its Catholic identity, the college provides a learning environment where students develop intellectually, spiritually, socially, and professionally.

We believe that education goes beyond the classroom. Through meaningful instruction, values formation, student organizations, and community involvement, KCC helps shape well-rounded individuals ready to contribute to society.

  • Academic excellence grounded in faith
  • Holistic student development
  • Strong commitment to the community
